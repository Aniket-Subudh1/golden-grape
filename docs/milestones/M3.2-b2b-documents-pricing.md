## Goal

`pricing.quote` implements every step of `04-DATA-MODEL.md` §6, so the webshop, a mobile sale and a quotation produce the same price for the same customer. B2B customers get their price lists (per bottle or per box). Staff turn a quotation into an order and then an invoice in one action. Invoices, delivery notes and credit notes are PDFs in the customer's language with gapless numbers and a VAT breakdown. Loyalty points and gift cards work across channels.

## In scope / out of scope

- **In:** the tasks below.
- **Out:** Peppol/UBL, dunning, statements, credit limits, accounting links, loyalty tiers and referrals, physical gift cards (Q§13). wallee payment links on invoices and quotations (M4.1).

## Tasks

### T1 · Price lists and B2B customers
Files: `modules/promotions/collections.ts` (`priceLists`), `modules/customers/collections.ts` (fields `priceList`, `paymentTerm`).

- `priceLists` per `04-DATA-MODEL.md` §5 (lines per variant and optional unit, so box prices are possible). The admin edits lines in an array field. For lists over 30 lines, use `scripts/import-pricelist.ts` (CSV: `sku, unit_factor, price_eur`).
- B2B import (DEP-08): `scripts/import-customers.ts` (CSV: company, VAT, contact, email, phone, address, group, price list, payment term). It is idempotent by email and capped at 30 rows per the contract (Q§12), which is not enforced in code.

### T2 · Promotions and discount codes
Files: `modules/promotions/collections.ts`.

- `promotions` and `discountCodes` per the data model. Codes are stored upper-cased and trimmed, and the `uses` counter can only be changed by the server.

### T3 · Loyalty and gift cards
Files: `modules/promotions/{loyalty,giftcards}.ts`, `modules/settings/globals.ts` (`loyalty` group), `modules/notifications/templates/gift-card.tsx`.

- Settings: `pointsPerEuro` (int), `pointValueCents`, `expiryMonths`, `eligibleChannels`.
- **Earn** (on the transition to `completed`): `points = floor(eligibleBase / 100 × pointsPerEuro)`, where `eligibleBase` = Σ totals of lines whose product is not `loyaltyExcluded` and is not a gift card, minus the loyalty value used, floored at 0. Only for a customer with an account, on an eligible channel. Write a `loyaltyLedger` row with `expiresAt` and `$inc` the balance. **Reverse** on `refunded` with a negative `adjust` row referencing the order.
- **Redeem** happens inside the order transaction: a conditional `$inc` (`loyaltyBalance ≥ points`) + a ledger row.
- **Expiry**: a cron job `loyalty-expire` (daily) writes `expire` rows for earn rows past `expiresAt` that are not yet consumed. The consumption order is FIFO. It is implemented in the pure `loyalty.ts` (`expirable(rows, now)`) and covered by vectors.
- Staff adjust with a reason: an admin button on the customer view.
- **Gift cards**: a product with `giftCard: true`, whose variants are the amounts (25/50/100 €) and have `trackStock: false`. When a gift-card line's order becomes paid or confirmed, the server issues one card per unit (secure code, 16 chars from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789`), writes the `issue` ledger row, and e-mails the code + QR (`qrcode` → data URL) to the recipient or buyer in their locale. Staff can also issue a card in the admin (button) with an amount and expiry. A redeem is a conditional `$inc` on `balanceCents ≥ amount` + ledger row. On a refund of an order that used a card, a `refund` row restores the balance.
- Customer account page: points balance + history, gift card balance lookup by code.

### T4 · `quote` full implementation (the core of this milestone)
Files: `modules/promotions/pricing.ts` (+ test), `modules/promotions/service.ts`.

- Implement steps 1–8 of `04-DATA-MODEL.md` §6. Keep it pure: `service.ts` loads the price lists (customer, then group), active promotions and code, the loyalty balance, gift cards and settings, then calls `quote`.
- `redeem(tx, quote, order)` runs inside the order transaction. It increments the code `uses` (conditional), redeems loyalty and gift cards (conditional), and throws `CONFLICT` if any conditional update matched nothing (for example, a card spent concurrently). `placeOrder` and `placeStaffOrder` call it. There is no other path.
- Checkout UI: add "Discount code", "Use my points" and "Gift card" fields (cart sheet and checkout), each re-quoting live. Mobile sale: the same three, plus the customer's list prices, which happen automatically via `quote`.

### T5 · Documents and numbering
Files: `modules/documents/{collections,service,actions}.ts`, `modules/documents/pdf/{layout,invoice,quotation,delivery-note,credit-note}.tsx`.

- Collections per `04-DATA-MODEL.md` §5 + `documentFiles`. Access makes invoices and credit notes immutable after issue (only `status`, `paidAt` and `payment` can be updated).
- `createQuotation(customer, lines, validUntil)` (staff; the price comes from `quote` with channel `mobile`). `sendQuotation` → status `sent` + e-mail with the PDF attached. `acceptQuotation` (staff, or the customer via a signed link in the e-mail) → in one transaction, `placeStaffOrder` from the quotation lines **at the quoted prices** (a quote is a commitment while valid; the order stores the quotation's `quote` snapshot instead of re-quoting) + status `accepted`. An expired quotation cannot be accepted. A cron job `quotations-expire` runs daily.
- `issueInvoice(orderId)`: in one transaction, `nextNumber('invoice', year)` → insert the invoice from the order snapshot → render the PDF → store it in `documentFiles` → link it. If rendering fails, the transaction rolls back and the number is not consumed. Due date = issue date + payment term days. `markInvoicePaid` (staff) sets `paidAt`.
- `issueDeliveryNote(orderId)` (no prices) and `issueCreditNote(invoiceId, lines, reason)`: lines and amounts ≤ the remaining creditable amount, invoice status → `credited` or `partly_credited`. The `refunded` order transition creates the credit note automatically when the order has an invoice.
- PDF: one shared `layout.tsx` (logo, business details and VAT number from private settings, customer billing snapshot, number, dates, the lines table, VAT breakdown per rate, totals, payment terms, legal mentions from settings, IBAN). All labels come from the `documents.*` dictionary namespace in the document's `locale`. Amounts use `formatMoney`.
- Mobile sale "Invoice later" (M3.1) → staff "Issue invoice" button on the order (admin + `/staff/orders`). The invoice e-mail goes to the customer with the PDF.
- `exportDocuments(range)` → CSV for the accountant (number, date, customer, VAT number, net per rate, VAT per rate, total, status). It is a server action that returns a file.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-M3.2-01 [R5]** Given a B2B customer with a price list, when they log in, then the shop shows their prices, and a quotation for them uses the same prices.
- **AC-M3.2-02 [R5]** Given an accepted quotation, when staff click "Create order" and then "Issue invoice", then an order and an invoice exist with the quoted prices, and the invoice PDF is in the customer's language.
- **AC-M3.2-03 [R5]** Given any sequence of issues, including a failed issue and concurrent issues, then invoice numbers are sequential per year with no gaps or duplicates, and an issued invoice cannot be edited or deleted by any role.
- **AC-M3.2-04 [R5]** Given a refund of an invoiced order, then a credit note is issued, numbered in its own series, and the invoice is marked credited.
- **AC-M3.2-05 [R7]** Given an active 10 % promotion on a brand, then the webshop, a mobile sale and a quotation for the same private customer produce the same totals.
- **AC-M3.2-06 [R7]** Given a discount code with min 50 € and max 1 use per customer, then it is refused below 50 € and on a second order by the same customer.
- **AC-M3.2-07 [R7]** Given a 50 € gift card, when it is used for 30 € and then 20 €, then the balance is 0 and a third use is refused.
- **AC-M3.2-08 [R7]** Given a completed order of 100 € with `pointsPerEuro 1`, then 100 points are added to the customer's balance and history, and refunding the order removes them.
- **AC-M3.2-09 [R12] [R5]** Given a B2B list price per box of 6, then a mobile sale of 2 boxes uses that price and removes 12 bottles.
- **AC-M3.2-10 [V-I18N]** Quotations, invoices, delivery notes and credit notes render in FR, EN, DE and PT with the correct VAT breakdown.
- **AC-M3.2-11 [V-NOTIF]** Sending a quotation and issuing an invoice each send one e-mail with the PDF attached.
