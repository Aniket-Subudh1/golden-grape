## Goal

The public shop and Counter, with real data, filters and search. A side cart priced **only by the server** (`pricing.quote` v1: base price + VAT). Customer sign-up and login, including business accounts. The full SEO layer. After M2.2 a visitor can browse in any language, fill the cart, see VAT-inclusive totals that match the server, and create an account. A customer can never see another customer's data.

## In scope / out of scope

- **In:** the tasks below.
- **Out:** checkout and order placement (M2.3), promotions, codes, B2B prices, loyalty and gift cards (M3.2: `quote` v1 has the full signature but implements steps 1 (public price only) and 5 of `04-DATA-MODEL.md` §6).

## Tasks

### T1 · Pricing engine v1
Files: `modules/promotions/pricing.ts` (+ test), `modules/promotions/service.ts`.

- Pure `quote(input)` with the **final** input and output types from `04-DATA-MODEL.md` §6. v1 implements: line price = packaging price, or `factor × variant.priceCents`; quantity; the VAT breakdown per rate (step 5); `amountDueCents = total`. The other steps are no-ops that M3.2 fills in. Writing the types now means no caller changes later.
- `service.quoteCart(lines, { customer?, channel, locale })` loads variants, units and VAT rates, calls `quote`, and returns lines with localized names, images and availability.
- Vectors: 3 × 1999 at 17 % → VAT 871; mixed 17 % and 3 % lines → two breakdown rows whose sum equals the total VAT; box6 at its own price beats `6 × unit`.

### T2 · Shop and Counter pages
Files: `app/(site)/[locale]/shop/page.tsx`, `shop/[slug]/page.tsx`, `snack/page.tsx`, `modules/catalogue/components/{product-card,menu-card,filters}.tsx`.

- **Shop listing**: port `shop.tsx`. Category chips are `Button variant="chip"` and come from `categories` (`kind: shop`). Search is the `q` search param, run server-side against the text index (debounced client-side `router.replace` keeps the URL as the state, so links can be shared). Extra filters live in a `Sheet` on mobile and inline on desktop: region, colour, price range. Results are paginated at 24 per page, and the empty state uses the prototype's `shop.empty` text.
- `ProductCard` = `MediaCard ratio="4/5"` + tag badge + add button (the `+` becomes `✓` once in the cart, as in the prototype). Out of stock: the button is disabled and the "Out of stock" label comes from the dictionary.
- **Product page** (new; the prototype has none, so build it from the same vocabulary): `next/image` gallery, `PageHero`-style title, wine attributes table, a packaging selector (bottle / box of 6 / box of 12 with their prices), `QtyStepper` with max = `availability.maxQty`, add to cart, and related products from the same category (4).
- **Counter / Snack**: port `snack.tsx`, with products of `kind: snack`, `MenuCard` = `MediaCard ratio="4/3"` + `QtyStepper`, the 3 CTA buttons opening the cart in a mode (`openAs`), allergens shown.
- `lib/brand.ts` is deleted at the end of this task, once nothing imports it (M1.2 T6).

### T3 · Cart
Files: `modules/webshop/cart.tsx` (provider), `components/site/{cart-sheet,floating-cart}.tsx`, `modules/webshop/actions.ts` (`quoteCartAction`).

- Client state keeps only `{ variant, unit?, qty }[]` + `mode`, persisted in localStorage `gg_cart_v2`. Drop `v1` data, which held client prices.
- When the sheet opens and after each change (debounced 300 ms), call `quoteCartAction`, which returns lines (name, image, unit price, line total, available qty) and totals. **The UI shows only server numbers.** Lines above the available stock are clamped, with a notice.
- Port the `CartSheet` look: mode switch, line list with `QtyStepper`, totals with VAT "incl." The contact and slot form part of the sheet becomes the checkout step in M2.3. In M2.2 the sheet ends with a "Checkout" button that links to `/[locale]/checkout`, and that page is built in M2.3, so the button is hidden until then.
- Port `FloatingCart` (shown only when count > 0, as in the prototype).
- Nav: show the cart, account and search icons (hidden in M1.2).

### T4 · Customer accounts
Files: `modules/customers/{collections,actions,service}.ts`, `app/(site)/[locale]/auth/page.tsx`, `account/{page,orders,addresses,profile}/page.tsx`.

- `customers` (Payload auth collection, its own cookie) and `customerGroups`, as `04-DATA-MODEL.md` §3, with field-level access so a customer cannot set `group`, `priceList`, `paymentTerm` or `loyaltyBalance`.
- Port `auth.tsx` (sign in / sign up tabs). Sign-up asks for type private or business; business requires `company` and `vatNumber`, the latter validated as a format check by country prefix only (no VIES call; out of scope). Forgot and reset password use Payload's built-in flow, with the e-mail template in the shared layout and the customer's locale.
- Rate limits: login 5 per 15 min per email+IP; sign-up 5 per hour per IP.
- `/account`: profile, addresses (CRUD, one default), order history (empty until M2.3 places orders; the route exists but its nav link is shown only when the customer has orders). The account layout redirects to `/auth` if not logged in.
- Guest checkout stays possible (M2.3). An account is optional.

### T5 · SEO layer
Files: `app/sitemap.ts`, `app/robots.ts`, `modules/catalogue/seo.ts`.

- `generateMetadata` on shop, product and snack pages: title, description, OG image (first product image), canonical, `alternates`.
- JSON-LD: `Product` + `Offer` (price, currency EUR, availability) on product pages; `LocalBusiness`/`WineStore` (address, hours from settings, geo) on home and contact; `BreadcrumbList` on product pages. Use one `JsonLd` component.
- `sitemap.ts`: every public route × 4 locales, with `alternates.languages`, including published products, events, pages and legal. `robots.ts`: disallow `/admin`, `/staff`, `/account`, `/checkout`, `/api`.

### T6 · Tests
- Unit: `pricing.test.ts` v1 vectors (T1).
- Integration (`modules/customers/access.int.test.ts`): with two customers A and B, A's local API calls with `overrideAccess: false, user: A` on `customers` (find, findByID B) and `orders` return no B data, and a REST `GET /api/customers/<B>` with A's cookie returns 403 or 404. A customer updating their own `priceList` is ignored or forbidden.
- No UI tests in this milestone (the checkout e2e in M2.3 covers the cart).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-M2.2-01 [V-SHOP] [V-I18N]** Given the seeded catalogue, when the shop is filtered by "Port" and searched for "tawny" in EN, then only matching products show, with EN names, and the URL reflects both filters.
- **AC-M2.2-02 [V-CART]** Given a cart with a box of 6 and 2 snacks, then the totals and the VAT breakdown shown equal `quote()` for those lines, and editing localStorage prices changes nothing displayed.
- **AC-M2.2-03 [V-CART] [R1]** Given a variant with 2 in stock, then the stepper cannot exceed 2, and a cart line of 5 is clamped to 2 with a notice.
- **AC-M2.2-04 [V-ACC]** Given a business sign-up without a VAT number, then it is rejected on the field; with one, the account is created as `business`.
- **AC-M2.2-05 [V-ACC]** Given customers A and B, then A can never read B's profile, addresses or orders via pages, actions or REST.
- **AC-M2.2-06 [V-SEO]** Given a product page, then it has a canonical URL, 4 hreflang alternates, OG tags, and valid `Product` JSON-LD (Rich Results Test, manual check).
- **AC-M2.2-07 [V-SEO]** `sitemap.xml` lists every published product in 4 locales; `robots.txt` blocks the private paths.
